Questions people ask

How many electrical contractors are registered in Des Moines?

11. That is every business the state currently lists with an active registration for this trade and a Des Moines address. The state confirms a current bond for 7 of them and current liability insurance for 0. The count changes as registrations are filed and lapse, and this page is rebuilt against the registry every week.

What do licensed, bonded, and insured mean?

Licensed means the business is registered with Washington's Department of Labor & Industries. Bonded means a surety company has promised to pay up to a set amount if the contractor fails you and you win a claim. Insured means a liability policy is on file to cover damage they cause. Three separate things, and a contractor can have one without the others.

How big is the bond?

Washington requires $30,000 from a general contractor and $15,000 from a specialty contractor. Those amounts rose on July 1, 2024; before that they had been $12,000 and $6,000 for twenty-two years. The median bond among the electrical contractors on this page is $4,000. A contractor who posts more than required is worth a second look.

Is the bond enough to cover my job?

Usually not. On a kitchen that costs $90,000, a $30,000 bond covers a third of it. The bond is also shared: if several homeowners file claims against the same contractor, they line up against one pot of money, and lawyers and suppliers can be in that line too. Treat the bond as a floor, not as protection. Our bond calculator shows the gap for your job.

Does a license mean they do good work?

No, and this is the thing most people get wrong. Registration means the paperwork is filed and the bond is posted. Nobody from the state watched anyone build anything. It is a floor to stand on, not a mark of skill. For credentials that are graded by people who inspect the work, see the certification guide.

How do I check a contractor myself?

Go to L&I's verify tool and search the business name or license number. Every listing on this page links straight to its record there. Do it the day you sign, not the day you read this — a registration can lapse in between. You can also use our one-box lookup.

Which electrical contractors have been registered the longest?

The longest-standing active registration on this page belongs to Fredhoes General Electric LLC, registered since 2007. Sort the list by how long a business has been registered and you get a rough sense of who has been around. It is a weak signal on its own — a long registration says a company kept paying its fees, not that its work is good.

Do these contractors only work in Des Moines?

No. The state records a mailing address, not a service area. A roofer with a Des Moines address will drive across the county, and one two towns over may well take your job. Use the city as a starting point, not a boundary.
