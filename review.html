Review a contractor

Six marks, one for each part of the job. Leave anything blank you cannot answer. We will set it aside.

Read this first.

The scale is tight on purpose. 3 means the contractor did what they promised, and that is a good score. If every job gets five stars, the number tells the next person nothing.

5
Exceptional. Better than the trade expects. Rare.
4
Better than you hoped for.
3
Did the job, did it right, did what was agreed.
2
Real trouble, but it got fixed.
1
A serious failure. Work you had to redo or fight about.
Cancel

Write only about work you paid for and saw yourself. There is a real business on the other end. A made-up or exaggerated review can cost someone their work. Be fair and be accurate. © .
