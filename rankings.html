Rankings & awards

Ranked by score, trade by trade. Most contractors land between 3.0 and 3.5, and a 3.5 is good work. Awards go to the top of the field: Gold to the top 1 percent, Silver the top 5, Bronze the top 15.

Why these numbers look low

A scale where everyone scores 4.7 tells you nothing. Four things keep this one useful:

  1. Scores start near the middle. One five-star review gives about 3.46. Getting to 4.0 takes good work over many jobs. A few reviews from friends will not buy a place on this list.
  2. Everyone is measured against everyone else. People rate contractors generously. We set the typical King County contractor near 3.2 and place the rest around him.
  3. Old reviews fade. Weight halves every four years. The crew from 2019 may be gone.
  4. Thin records are never ranked. Under three reviews we show a score, mark it as thin, and keep it off every list on this page.

These rankings come from reviews written here and nowhere else. That is a small pool. A contractor missing from a list has probably just not been reviewed yet. © .
